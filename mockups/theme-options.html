Interactive effect mockup

What happens when someone clicks

Click each button for real, more than once. This is the "solar" interaction most panel installers reach for: something that reads as light or energy releasing from the point you touched. All three are built from the same trick real solar sites use: cheap CSS transforms, never a heavy animation library.

Effect 1

Sunburst rays

Thin rays of light fire outward from the click, like a lens catching the sun. Reads as "energy arriving."

Effect 2

Photon scatter

A small scatter of warm particles flies outward and fades, like photons knocked loose. Quieter, more playful.

Effect 3

Energy ripple

A ring of energy pulses outward and a soft glow blooms behind it, like a panel converting one pulse of sunlight.

My default pick, if you want one: Option A for the palette (safest, keeps the site's existing personality, lowest risk of it reading as a stock "eco" template) and Effect 3 for clicks (calmest, reads as energy rather than a firework, easiest to use everywhere without becoming annoying on the fortieth click). But say which you actually like and I build from that instead.
