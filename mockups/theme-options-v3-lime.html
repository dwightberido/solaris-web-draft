Round 3 — Option E direction, lime as the field color

Three ways to make lime the major color, not just the accent

You picked Option E's hue and Effect 2, and asked for the canvas itself to be bright lime rather than a dark background with a lime glow on top. These three take that literally, each a different amount of "bright."

One honest flag before you look: a fully light lime page (Option 1) is the boldest and most literal read of "not dark," but reads less like most other solar sites, which tend to keep dark or neutral backgrounds so panel photography looks rich against them. Options 2 and 3 keep lime dominant while giving photography somewhere darker to sit. Not telling you which to pick, just flagging the tradeoff before you look.

Option 1

Lime Daylight

A genuine light theme. Off-white canvas tinted faintly lime, dark charcoal-green text, and a saturated lime doing the button, the active nav, and the headline highlight. The boldest, most literal "not dark."
